Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy covers os-ui Calendar and Atlas (Life OS), personal apps used only by their owner.

What the apps access

With your permission, the apps use the Google Calendar API to read your calendar list and events, and to create, change or delete events when you ask them to.

Where your data goes

Calendar data is stored only on the computer where the app runs. It isn't sent to any server run by the developer, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The apps talk only to Google.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The apps' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect inside the app, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder removes the stored copy.
